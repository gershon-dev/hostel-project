Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UEW Campus Hostels ("HostelFinder", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website and booking services.

1. Information We Collect

When you make an enquiry or a booking, we collect:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number / Mobile Money number
  • Preferred hostel and room type
  • Any message or additional details you choose to provide

We do not collect or store your Mobile Money PIN, card details, or any full payment credentials — these are handled entirely and securely by our payment processor, Paystack.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your hostel booking
  • To communicate with you about your booking, enquiry, or accommodation
  • To coordinate move-in details with the relevant hostel operator
  • To respond to support requests and questions
  • To improve our platform and services

3. How We Store Your Information

Booking and enquiry data is stored securely using Supabase, a hosted database provider, with access restricted by row-level security policies. Only authorized administrators can view booking and enquiry records.

4. Payment Processing

Payments are processed by Paystack, a licensed and PCI-DSS compliant payment service provider. When you pay, you are redirected to Paystack's secure checkout; your payment details are handled directly by Paystack under its own privacy and security policies, not by us.

5. Sharing of Information

We share your booking details only with the specific partner hostel you book with, so they can prepare your room and confirm move-in. We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share data with service providers (such as Supabase and Paystack) strictly to operate the Platform.

6. Data Retention

We retain booking and enquiry records for as long as necessary to fulfil your accommodation, respond to support needs, and comply with any applicable legal or academic-year record-keeping requirements.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by us by contacting us using the details below, subject to any records we are required to retain for bookings already completed.

8. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies or browser storage required for core site functionality (such as keeping the booking form working). We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at hostels@uew.edu.gh.
