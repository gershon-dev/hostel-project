You are responsible for providing accurate personal details (name, email, phone number) when booking. We are not liable for booking issues arising from incorrect information supplied by you.

All payments are collected via Mobile Money (MTN Mobile Money, Vodafone Cash, or AirtelTigo Money) through Paystack, a licensed third-party payment processor. We do not store your Mobile Money PIN or full payment credentials on our servers.

4. No Refund Policy

All bookings and payments made through this Platform are final and strictly non-refundable. Once a payment is successfully processed for a room, no refund, partial refund, credit, or exchange will be issued for any reason, including but not limited to: cancellation, change of mind, no-show, early departure, or failure to complete registration at the University.

By completing a booking and payment, you acknowledge and accept this no-refund policy. We strongly encourage you to confirm room type, price, and hostel details carefully before making payment.

In the rare event that a room becomes unavailable after payment due to an error on our part (e.g. double-booking caused by a system error), we will work with you to offer an equivalent alternative room. Where no suitable alternative exists, resolution will be handled at our discretion on a case-by-case basis.
