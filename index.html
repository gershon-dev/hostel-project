About Us

Who We Are

UEW Campus Hostels (operating online as HostelFinder) is a student accommodation booking platform serving students of the University of Education, Winneba. We work directly with a small number of trusted, on-campus and near-campus hostel operators — including Guest Hall, SSNIT Hostel, and Finess Hostel — to make finding and securing a bed space simple, transparent, and stress-free.

Our platform lets you browse real hostels, compare room types and prices, and book your space online with secure Mobile Money payment through Paystack — no more queuing, no more guesswork, no more missed rooms.

We are a Ghana-based student services initiative built by and for the UEW community, and we're committed to fair pricing, honest listings, and responsive support throughout your stay.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Everything you need to know about booking and paying for your hostel accommodation.

Browse the hostels listed on our Hostels section, click on a hostel to view its available room types, then click "Book This Room" on the option you want. Fill in your details and complete payment via Mobile Money through Paystack to confirm your booking.

We accept Mobile Money payments (MTN Mobile Money, Vodafone Cash, and AirtelTigo Money) processed securely through Paystack. We do not accept cash payments through the platform.

Yes. All payments are processed by Paystack, a licensed and PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. We never see or store your Mobile Money PIN or full payment credentials.

All bookings are final and non-refundable. Once a payment is successfully made for a room, that room is reserved for you and no refund, credit, or exchange will be issued for cancellations, no-shows, or change of mind. Please see our Terms of Service for full details before booking.

Each room type displays a live availability status — green means spaces are available, yellow means limited spaces remain, and red means the room is fully booked. Availability updates as bookings come in.

Once Paystack confirms your payment, your booking is recorded and secured against that room. You'll be able to move in on the agreed date coordinated with the hostel management — reach out to us via the Contact section if you need to confirm move-in details.

Room changes are handled on a case-by-case basis and depend on availability. Since bookings are non-refundable, contact us as soon as possible if you need to discuss a change, and we'll do our best to help — though a swap is not guaranteed.
